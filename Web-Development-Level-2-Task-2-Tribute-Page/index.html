Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan, Pakistani scientist and engineer

HIS STORY

Biography

Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan was a Pakistani metallurgical engineer and scientist. He was born on 1 April 1936 in Bhopal, India, and later migrated to Pakistan. His education and scientific career developed around engineering and metallurgy.

During his professional career, Dr. Khan worked in Europe and developed expertise in metallurgical engineering. His technical experience later became connected with Pakistan's nuclear development program.

Dr. Khan became one of the most recognized scientists in Pakistan because of his involvement in the country's uranium-enrichment program. His work became an important part of Pakistan's scientific and technological history.

Beyond his scientific career, Dr. Khan was also known for supporting educational institutions and scientific activities. He established educational initiatives and remained a prominent public figure in Pakistan until his death in 2021.
